Dimensionality reduction refers to the method of reducing the amount of original variables
by creating components of existing variables.

The most famous algorithm for dimensionality reduction is PCA (Principal Component Analysis),
which converts the original variables into principal components. One principal component is a 
weighted combination of the original variables.

In a nutshell, with PCA, we can reduce our original amount of variables (e.g. 50 variables) 
into 15 principal components, for example.
