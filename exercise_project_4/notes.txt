NOISE AND OUTLIERS

This folder will contain examples on how to use some of the most 
common noise and outlier detection/removal tools. 

For noise:
- MAD (Mean Absolute Deviation)
- RANSAC (Random Sample Consensus) (also removes some outliers)

For outliers:
- The basics: IQR, z-score
- Isolation Forest
- Elliptic Envelope
- LOF (Local Outlier Factor)
- Clustering approaches: DBSCAN
